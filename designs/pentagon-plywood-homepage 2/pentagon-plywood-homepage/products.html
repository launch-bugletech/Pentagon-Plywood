A Simple Starting Point

Three questions before
you choose.

If the answer is not obvious, share the application, quantity and location with our team. We will help narrow the selection.

  1. 01

    Where will it be used?

    Dry interior, moisture-prone room, structural furniture component or finished doorway.

  2. 02

    What will it become?

    Cabinetry, wardrobe, shelf, partition, counter, door or another built element.

  3. 03

    What must be verified?

    Grade, thickness, applicable standard, availability, quantity and delivery location.
