01 · MR Grade Plywood

Dependable material for
everyday interiors.

MR grade plywood is intended for furniture and interior applications where exposure is generally dry, with occasional humidity or minor moisture conditions. It is commonly considered for residential and commercial interior work.

Typical uses

  • Wardrobes and bedroom furniture
  • Office furniture and partitions
  • Wall panelling and interior fixtures
  • Shelves, cabinets and storage units

Product highlights

  • Made for dry interior applications
  • Available in practical thickness options
  • Suitable for furniture and fit-out work
  • Bulk and project enquiries supported

Marine / BWP
Moisture-prone applications

02 · Marine Plywood

Specified where moisture
demands more.

Marine plywood is positioned for interior applications where recurring moisture exposure calls for a BWP-grade material. It is a common starting point for kitchens, utility areas and other demanding cabinetry environments.

Typical uses

  • Kitchen cabinetry and base units
  • Utility areas and moisture-prone storage
  • Bathroom furniture away from direct immersion
  • Commercial counters and service areas

Product highlights

  • BWP grade positioning
  • Phenolic resin bonding reference
  • Selected for higher moisture exposure
  • Project and bulk enquiries supported
