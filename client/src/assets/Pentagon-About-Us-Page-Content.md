# Pentagon Plywood — About Us Page Content

**Breadcrumb:**  
Home › About Us

## Important Content Direction

The About Us page should not read like another product page. Its purpose is to answer:

- Who is Pentagon Plywood?
- How long has the company been operating?
- What does it manufacture?
- What happens behind the finished board?
- What does the company value?
- Why should a dealer, builder, architect or customer trust the business?

The current website confirms that Pentagon Plywood Pvt. Ltd. was established in 1997, operates from Yamunanagar, is led by Mr. Anand Jain, manufactures and supplies wood-panel products, has manufacturing infrastructure and an in-house R&D unit, and states that quality checks take place throughout production.

The following page uses those facts without repeating unsupported superlatives such as “leading,” “peerless” or “unmatched.”

By the time a finished panel reaches its last check, many decisions have already shaped it. The condition of the material, core construction, adhesive, pressure, temperature, thickness and finishing all influence the result.

- Moisture-content checks
- Core and dimensional inspection
- Bonding and pressing controls
- Thickness and size verification
- Surface and edge inspection
- Product testing according to the applicable grade
- Batch identification and record keeping
