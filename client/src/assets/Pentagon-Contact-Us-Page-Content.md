# Pentagon Plywood — Contact Us Page Content

## Recommended page purpose

The Contact Us page should help visitors do one of four things quickly:

1. Request product information or a quotation.
2. Discuss a dealer or distributor opportunity.
3. Share a bulk, construction, interior or institutional requirement.
4. Call, email, WhatsApp or find Pentagon's location.

The current page collects basic product, quantity and contact details, but it does not help Pentagon understand what type of enquiry has arrived. The redesigned page should begin with the enquiry purpose and then show relevant fields.

- a real Pentagon team or office photograph;
- a genuine factory entrance photograph; or
- a clean product composition showing plywood, blockboard and a finished door.

### Form experience rules

- Keep only 6–8 fields visible initially.
- Reveal technical or business fields according to the enquiry type.

- Make business email compulsory for export enquiries.

- Add spam protection that does not interrupt genuine visitors.
- Send an acknowledgement email or WhatsApp message only after Pentagon confirms the workflow and sender account.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I request a product quotation?

Select “Product Enquiry” or “Project or Bulk Requirement” in the form and share the product, size, thickness, quantity and delivery location. If you do not know every specification, describe the intended application.

### Can I send my requirement on WhatsApp?

Yes. The currently published WhatsApp number is +91 72061 04340. Include the product, quantity and delivery city, and attach any relevant reference image or document.

### Can I enquire about becoming a dealer or distributor?

Yes. Select “Dealer or Distributor Enquiry” and provide your business name, market location, current product categories and territory.

### Can architects, builders and interior designers submit a BOQ?

The redesigned form should allow project buyers and professionals to upload a BOQ, specification, drawing or reference document. Pentagon must confirm the accepted file types and maximum upload size.

### Can I visit the Pentagon manufacturing location?

The published address is Village Raipur, Khajuri Road, Yamunanagar, Haryana 135001. Contact the team before visiting so the timing and appropriate representative can be confirmed.

### Does Pentagon accept export enquiries?

The existing website describes Pentagon as a manufacturer and supplier but does not clearly confirm current export operations. The page may collect export enquiries, but it should not claim active export markets until Pentagon confirms them.

### What information should I include in my enquiry?

Where possible, include the product, grade, size, thickness, quantity, application, delivery location and required-by date.

### When will I receive a response?

Pentagon should provide a realistic response-time commitment before the website publishes one. Until then, use: “Our team will review your requirement and contact you using your preferred method.”
